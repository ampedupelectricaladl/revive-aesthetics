Section 2

What to be aware of

Your treatment involves the following. Please read before you continue.

  • ◆
    Chemical lifting and setting solutions applied to your lashes The lash lift uses a thioglycolate-based cream to reshape the lash, followed by a setting solution. These are applied to the lashes and sit close to the eyelid skin. Although contact with the eye is avoided, fumes in a small space may cause mild nausea, which can be more pronounced during pregnancy.
  • ◆
    Lash and brow tint containing hydrogen peroxide Both the lash tint and brow tint are applied to the lash line and brow area. The developer used is low-volume hydrogen peroxide. While applied externally, you should be aware this is a chemical dye applied to your face.
  • ◆
    Heightened chemical sensitivity during pregnancy Pregnancy hormones can increase the skin's sensitivity to products. There is a slightly higher chance of irritation, redness, or unexpected reactions compared to a non-pregnant client. If at any point during the treatment you feel uncomfortable, please tell Stefani immediately.
  • ◆
    Lying on your back for approximately 2 hours The full lash lift and brow treatment requires you to remain flat on your back for around 2 hours. In later pregnancy this can cause discomfort or dizziness. Stefani can adjust your position and take breaks if needed — please speak up if you feel unwell at any point.
  • ◆
    Stefani is a beauty therapist, not a medical professional Revive Aesthetics offers cosmetic beauty treatments only. The decision to go ahead with this treatment during pregnancy is yours. Stefani recommends you consult your GP or midwife if you have any concerns before attending.
