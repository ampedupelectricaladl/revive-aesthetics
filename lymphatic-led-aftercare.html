Post-Treatment Guide

Your body has just been given a beautiful reset. The hours after your treatment are when the magic really happens — give it the support it needs and you will feel and see the difference.

Stefani x

After your treatment
Right away — most important
  • ✓ Drink at least 2 litres of water today — this is the single most important thing you can do. Lymphatic drainage moves fluid and waste through the body, and water helps flush it out properly
  • ✓ You may notice you need to use the bathroom more than usual — this is completely normal and a sign the treatment is working
  • ✕ Avoid alcohol for at least 24 hours — it dehydrates and burdens the lymphatic system, counteracting the treatment
  • ✕ Skip heavy, processed, or salty food today — keep meals light and nourishing to let your body continue the detox process
First 12–24 hours
  • ✕ Avoid strenuous exercise — a gentle walk is wonderful and encouraged, but skip anything high-intensity for the rest of the day
  • ✕ Avoid tight waistbands, shapewear, or anything restrictive around the stomach and waist — this can restrict lymphatic flow
  • ✕ Limit caffeine if you can — it dehydrates and can disrupt the calming effect of the treatment
  • ✓ Rest if you feel tired or deeply relaxed — some clients feel quite sleepy or even slightly emotional after a lymphatic massage. This is a normal detox response, not a cause for concern
  • ✓ LED aftercare: some mild warmth, redness, or flushing on the skin is completely normal and will settle quickly. Apply SPF if you are heading outdoors

Getting the most from your results

💧 Keep up your water intake for the next 2–3 days. Hydration is the single biggest factor in how long the benefits of lymphatic drainage last.
🚶 Gentle movement and walking every day helps keep the lymphatic system active. Even 20 minutes a day makes a real difference between sessions.
🌿 Reduce salt and processed foods where you can — excess sodium causes fluid retention and works against the drainage effect.
✨ LED light therapy works cumulatively. A course of treatments gives the best results for skin rejuvenation, tone, and inflammation. Ask Stefani about the right schedule for your skin goals.
📅 For lymphatic drainage, a session every 2–4 weeks is ideal for ongoing wellbeing. More frequent sessions can be helpful during periods of stress, bloating, or skin congestion.
Some people experience a brief detox response in the 24–48 hours after treatment — mild fatigue, light-headedness, or feeling slightly under the weather. This is temporary and a sign the treatment is doing its work. Drink plenty of water, rest, and it will pass. If anything concerns you, reach out.
