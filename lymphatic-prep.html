Your Prep Guide

A few simple things to do before your appointment to help your body get the most out of the session.

Hi — can't wait to see you! Lymphatic drainage is wonderfully relaxing, and a little preparation helps your body respond even better. Here's what to know before you arrive. Stefani x
The day before

Hydrate & settle

  • ✓ Drink at least 8 glasses of water throughout the day — hydration is the single most important thing you can do to support lymphatic drainage
  • ✓ Keep your meals light and easy to digest — heavy, rich meals can work against drainage
  • ✕ Limit alcohol — it dehydrates the body and can cause extra fluid retention the next day
  • ✕ Avoid very salty foods that cause your body to hold excess water
On the day

Arrive comfortable

  • ✓ Wear loose, comfortable clothing — you'll need to comfortably expose your stomach area for the treatment
  • ✓ Have a light meal at least 2 hours before your appointment — a full stomach makes the treatment less comfortable
  • ✓ Keep drinking water — even a glass or two before you leave home helps
  • ✕ Skip heavy exercise within 2 hours of your appointment — your body needs to be in a calm, rested state
  • ✕ Avoid caffeine just before if you tend to feel anxious with it — the treatment is deeply relaxing and you'll get more out of it in a calm state
Please let Stefani know if you are pregnant, or if you have any recent abdominal surgery, hernia, active inflammation, or blood clotting conditions — lymphatic massage has specific precautions for these.
After your session

Support the results

  • ✓ Drink plenty of water in the hours after — this is really important for flushing what the drainage has shifted
  • ✓ Keep meals light and nutritious for the rest of the day
  • ✕ Avoid strenuous exercise and alcohol for at least 24 hours
  • ✕ Skip heavy, processed or very salty foods the day of your appointment
Good to know

What to expect

🌿 The massage is gentle, slow and deeply relaxing — most clients describe it as almost meditative.
💧 You may feel the urge to use the bathroom more frequently in the hours after — this is completely normal and actually a great sign that drainage is happening.
🌀 Some mild bloating can occur right after before it reduces — this settles within a few hours for most people.
✨ Many clients notice an immediate feeling of lightness and reduced puffiness. Best and most lasting results come with a regular course of sessions.
😴 You may feel pleasantly tired afterwards — rest and take it easy for the evening if you can.
