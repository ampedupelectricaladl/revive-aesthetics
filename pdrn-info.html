The ingredient

What is PDRN?

PDRN stands for Polydeoxyribonucleotide — a highly purified extract that activates your skin's own repair and regeneration pathways. It's often called "salmon DNA" because of its biological origin, though it's fully purified and safe for all skin types.

When delivered into the skin during microneedling, PDRN stimulates collagen synthesis, reduces inflammation, accelerates cell turnover, and supports tissue healing — amplifying the results of the needling itself. It's what makes this treatment a "booster" rather than standard microneedling.

The session

What happens at your appointment

  1. 1Your skin is thoroughly cleansed and a topical numbing cream is applied and left to work for 20–30 minutes.
  2. 2The PDRN booster serum is applied to the treatment area.
  3. 3A microneedling device creates thousands of microscopic channels across the skin, driving the PDRN deep into the dermis and stimulating your skin's natural collagen response.
  4. 4A calming, hydrating mask is applied immediately after to soothe and begin the recovery process.
  5. 5Stefani reviews your skin and gives you your personalised aftercare instructions to take home.

Questions

Frequently asked

Does microneedling hurt?
A numbing cream is applied 20–30 minutes before treatment, so discomfort is minimal. Most clients describe the sensation as mild pressure or light scratching — very manageable. Sensitivity varies by area; around the forehead and nose bridge tends to be the most sensitive.
How much downtime should I expect?
Most clients are back to their regular routine within 24 hours, though redness can linger for up to 48 hours. Plan to avoid social events, sun exposure, and wearing makeup for at least the first 24 hours after your session.
How many sessions do I need?
A single session produces noticeable results, particularly for general skin health and glow. For significant scarring, deeper texture concerns, or firming, a course of 3 sessions (4–6 weeks apart) is recommended for transformative, lasting change.
What skincare should I avoid beforehand?
Stop using retinol, vitamin A, AHAs, BHAs, and exfoliating products 72 hours before your appointment. On the day, come with completely bare skin — no makeup, SPF, or serum. Your full prep guide will be sent before your appointment.
Can I wear makeup the same day?
No makeup for the first 24 hours after treatment. Your skin's barrier needs time to calm and close the microchannels. After 24 hours, a light, non-comedogenic mineral SPF and foundation are fine to resume.
Is PDRN safe?
Yes — PDRN is a well-researched, clinically used ingredient. It's fully purified and safe for all skin types, including sensitive skin. It has anti-inflammatory properties, meaning it actually helps calm the skin during the microneedling process rather than irritating it.
