Your Prep Guide

What to do (and avoid) before your brow appointment for the best possible result.

So excited to work on your brows! A little preparation makes a real difference — especially for lamination. Follow these steps and you'll come in with the ideal canvas for a beautiful result. Stefani x

1 week before

Give your brows a rest

  • ✕ No waxing, threading, or tweezing — let your natural brow hairs grow in fully so Stefani can map and shape them for your face
  • ✕ No brow tinting or colouring — a fresh tint works best on undyed hairs
  • ✕ No Botox or filler injections in the forehead or brow area within 2 weeks of your appointment
If you've had a brow lamination in the last 6 weeks, please let Stefani know before booking — your brow hairs need time to recover between treatments.
48 hours before

Things to avoid

  • ✕ Exfoliating scrubs, AHAs, BHAs, or retinol applied to the brow and forehead area
  • ✕ Chemical face peels or treatments — your skin barrier needs to be intact
  • ✕ Steam rooms, saunas, or activities that cause heavy sweating
  • ✓ Moisturise the brow area as normal — hydrated skin and hair respond better
On the day

Arrive ready

  • ✓ Come with clean brows and forehead — no brow pencil, pomade, powder, or product in the brow area
  • ✓ Your natural brow growth is your guide — resist tidying or plucking strays before you arrive
  • ✓ Bring or describe any inspo you love — Stefani will map your shape to your face, but it helps to see what you're drawn to
  • ✓ Let Stefani know about any skin sensitivities, conditions, or allergies — especially if you've ever had a reaction to tint or lamination products
  • ✓ If you're having the lash and brow combo, follow the lash prep guide too — no eye makeup and wear glasses instead of contacts
