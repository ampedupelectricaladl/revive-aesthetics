Your Prep Guide

Everything you need to know in the 24–48 hours before your appointment.

Hi gorgeous — so excited to see you at the studio! To make sure you get the very best result from your lash lift, here are a few simple things to do (and avoid) beforehand. It really does make a difference. Stefani x
48 hours before

Things to avoid

  • ✕ Waterproof mascara — it leaves a residue that blocks the lifting solution
  • ✕ Oil-based makeup removers or serums around the eye area
  • ✕ Eyelash curlers — your natural lash shape matters going in
  • ✕ Steam rooms, saunas, or intense sweating (gym, hot yoga)
  • ✕ Swimming — chlorine and salt water affect lash texture
If you wear lash extensions, please let Stefani know before your appointment — lash lifts are done on natural lashes only.
24 hours before

What helps

  • ✓ Wash your lashes gently with a mild, oil-free cleanser
  • ✓ Keep eye makeup minimal — regular mascara is fine to wear the day before
  • ✓ Let Stefani know about any allergies, sensitivities, or eye conditions
  • ✓ If you wear contacts, plan to switch to glasses on the day
On the day

Arrive ready

  • ✓ Come with clean, dry lashes — no mascara or eye makeup
  • ✓ Remove contact lenses before your appointment (bring your glasses)
  • ✓ Arrive a few minutes early so you can settle in and relax
  • ✓ Wear something comfortable — you'll be lying down for about an hour
