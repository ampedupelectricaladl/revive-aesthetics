Brow Lamination Aftercare

Post-Treatment Guide

Your brows have just been beautifully laminated, tinted and shaped. The lamination solution needs a little time to fully set — follow these guidelines and your results will last as long as possible.

Stefani x

After your treatment
First 24 hours — most important
  • ✕ Keep your brows completely dry — no washing the face over the brow area, no sweating, no swimming, no steam or humid environments
  • ✕ No makeup on the brows — avoid pencil, powder, pomade, brow gel, or any product directly on the brow hairs
  • ✕ Don't rub, touch, or scratch the brow area — the lamination is still setting and friction can break the shape
  • ✕ Avoid hot showers with steam on the face, saunas, and any heat directed near the brows
  • ✕ No exfoliating acids (AHAs, BHAs), retinol, or scrubs anywhere near the brow area
  • ✓ Leave your brows exactly as they are — the lamination sets best with no interference at all in the first 24 hours
24–48 hours
  • ✓ From 24 hours you can gently brush your brows upward into shape using a clean spoolie — do this morning and night
  • ✓ Brow makeup can be resumed after 24 hours
  • ✕ Continue to avoid waxing, threading, or tweezing stray hairs until at least 48 hours have passed
  • ✕ Still avoid exfoliating acids and retinol directly on or around the brow area for the full 48 hours

Ongoing care — for longevity

🌿 Brush your brows upward each morning with a clean spoolie. This is especially important in the first two weeks — it trains the hairs to stay in their lifted position.
💧 From day 2, apply a small amount of brow oil or castor oil to the hairs at night. This nourishes them after the lamination process and helps maintain softness and shape.
☀️ Protect your brows with SPF when in the sun — UV exposure fades a tint faster and can dry out the brow hairs over time.
✂️ Only tweeze any stray hairs once the lamination has fully set (after 2–3 days). Avoid heavy reshaping between appointments — let Stefani maintain the shape each visit.
📅 A brow lamination lasts 6–8 weeks. Come back no sooner than 6 weeks to keep the brow hairs healthy and strong. The tint will gradually fade over 3–5 weeks, more quickly with exfoliation and sun exposure.
Some mild redness or slight skin sensitivity around the brow area immediately after treatment is normal and will settle within a few hours. If you experience any unusual irritation, swelling, or a reaction that doesn't settle, please reach out straight away.
