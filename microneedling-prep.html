PDRN Microneedling

Your Prep Guide

What to do (and avoid) in the days before your session to get the very best result.

Hi — so excited to see you at the studio! Microneedling is one of the most effective skin treatments available, and a little preparation makes a real difference to your results and recovery. Here's everything you need to know. Stefani x
72 hours before

Pause your actives

  • ✕ Retinol, tretinoin, vitamin A or Differin — these thin the skin's barrier
  • ✕ AHAs and BHAs — glycolic, lactic, salicylic acid — stop all exfoliating acids
  • ✕ Exfoliating scrubs, peels, or enzyme masks
  • ✕ Waxing or threading the treatment area
  • ✕ Fake tan or spray tan on your face
  • ✕ Significant sun exposure — if you can't avoid it, wear SPF 50+ every day
If you're on Roaccutane / isotretinoin, or have taken it in the last 12 months, please let Stefani know as soon as possible — treatment timing matters.
24 hours before

Settle your skin

  • ✕ Heavy gym sessions and intense sweating — heat and sweat can irritate a sensitive skin barrier
  • ✕ Alcohol — it can dehydrate your skin and thin your blood slightly, affecting healing
  • ✕ Anti-inflammatory pain relief (ibuprofen, aspirin) — these can reduce the treatment's effectiveness
  • ✓ Stick to your gentle, hydrating products — a mild cleanser and moisturiser is perfect
  • ✓ Drink plenty of water — well-hydrated skin responds better and heals faster
On the day

Arrive fresh

  • ✓ Come with completely clean skin — no makeup, no SPF, no serums. Just cleansed.
  • ✓ Have a light meal before you come — don't arrive fasting
  • ✓ Stay well hydrated in the morning
  • ✕ Skip your regular actives that morning (vitamin C, niacinamide, acids)
Let Stefani know on the day if you have an active cold sore, open wound, sunburn, or active breakout in the treatment area — we may need to adjust your treatment plan.
Good to know

What to expect

🌿 Some redness and warmth immediately after is completely normal — most clients look and feel fine within 24 hours.
💧 Your skin may feel tight or look slightly flushed for up to 48 hours. Light flaking in the days after is also normal as your skin renews.
☀️ Avoid direct sun exposure for at least 72 hours after treatment, and wear SPF 50+ daily. Your skin is extra sensitive post-session.
✨ The PDRN (salmon DNA extract) in this treatment actively supports cell repair and collagen — results improve over 4–6 weeks as your skin rebuilds.
