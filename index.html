Questions

Frequently asked

Everything you might want to know before your first visit to the studio.

What treatments does Revive Aesthetics offer in Adelaide?

Revive Aesthetics is an Adelaide lash and brow studio. The menu is Korean lash lifts and tints, brow lamination, and brow shaping and tinting — on their own or combined in one visit. Skin treatments are also available: PDRN microneedling and LED light therapy.

What is a Korean lash lift?

A Korean lash lift is a semi-permanent treatment that lifts and curls your own natural lashes from the root — not the mid-shaft like a traditional perm. A tint is applied straight after to deepen the colour, so every lash is defined without mascara. No extensions are involved. Read the full guide.

How long does a lash lift last?

A Korean lash lift lasts 6 to 8 weeks, gradually relaxing as your natural lashes grow out. A full new lift is recommended no sooner than 6 weeks after your last one.

Can I have a lash lift if I have lash extensions?

Lash extensions need to be removed first. Once they are off, a Korean lash lift works on most natural lash types — short, long, straight or downward-pointing lashes all respond well.

How do I prepare for my lash or brow appointment?

For lashes, come with clean, dry lashes and no eye makeup, and avoid waterproof mascara, oil-based eye products and lash curlers in the 48 hours before. For brows, let your natural brow hairs grow in — no waxing, threading or tweezing beforehand. The full guides are here: lash prep and brow prep.

Where are you located, and when are you open?

Revive Aesthetics is at 262 Pulteney St in Adelaide's city centre, open Monday and Tuesday, 10am – 8pm, by appointment. You can book online anytime, or call 0404 967 051.

Do I need a consultation before my first treatment?

Lash and brow appointments can be booked online straight away. For skin treatments, every new client starts with a free skin consultation — it lets us assess your skin, understand your goals and recommend the safest, most effective plan for you.

What is PDRN microneedling?

PDRN microneedling combines collagen-induction microneedling with a PDRN (polynucleotide) booster that supports your skin's natural repair. It helps soften scarring, smooth texture and improve firmness for a fresher, plumper complexion.
