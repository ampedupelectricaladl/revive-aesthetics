Post-Treatment Aftercare

Korean Lash Lift

Your lashes have just been beautifully lifted and set. The next 24–48 hours are critical for locking in your results — please follow these guidelines carefully.

Recovery Timeline

0–24 hrs Keep lashes completely dry. No water, steam, sweat, or humidity. The lifting solution is still setting — any moisture now can cause lashes to drop or lose shape.
24–48 hrs Lashes are setting into their new shape. Continue to be gentle — avoid rubbing your eyes and sleep on your back where possible.
48–72 hrs Your lift is fully set. You can resume normal cleansing and carefully apply mascara if desired.
Week 2–8 Enjoy your lifted lashes! Nourish them regularly with a lash serum or conditioning oil to keep them healthy between appointments.

Do's & Don'ts

✓ Do

  • Keep lashes dry for the first 24 hours
  • Sleep on your back or use a silk pillowcase
  • Gently comb lashes upward daily with a spoolie
  • Use a nourishing lash serum or castor oil
  • Cleanse gently with an oil-free cleanser around the eyes
  • Apply mascara after 48 hours if desired

✕ Don't

  • Get lashes wet for the first 24 hours (no swimming, steam, sweating)
  • Rub or touch your eyes
  • Use oil-based makeup removers or cleansers on the eyes
  • Use waterproof mascara (very hard to remove without tugging)
  • Sleep face-down in the first 48 hours
  • Use an eyelash curler — your lashes are already lifted!

When to Contact Us

Some mild redness or sensitivity around the eye area is normal and will settle quickly. If you experience prolonged irritation, unusual swelling, or any reaction to the treatment, please get in touch straight away.
