Brow Aftercare

Post-Treatment Guide

Your brows look beautiful — now let's keep them that way. The next 24–48 hours are the most important for a long-lasting result.

Stefani x

After your treatment
First 24 hours — most important
  • ✕ Keep your brows completely dry — no washing the face over the brow area, no sweating, no swimming, no steam
  • ✕ No makeup on the brows — no pencil, powder, pomade, or brow gel for the first 24 hours
  • ✕ Don't rub, touch, or scratch the brow area — the lamination is still setting and any friction can disrupt the shape
  • ✕ Avoid saunas, hot showers with steam on the face, and any intense heat near the brow area
  • ✓ Leave your brows exactly as they are — the lamination sets best with no interference at all in the first day
24–48 hours
  • ✕ Continue to avoid exfoliating acids (AHAs, BHAs), retinol, or scrubs on or around the brow area
  • ✕ No waxing, threading, or tweezing any stray hairs — wait until the full 48 hours have passed
  • ✓ You can gently brush your brows up into position — use a clean spoolie brush from 24 hours onward
  • ✓ Brow makeup is fine to resume after 24 hours

Ongoing care — for longevity

🌿 Brush your brows into place each morning with a clean spoolie — this trains the hairs to stay lifted, especially in the first two weeks after a lamination.
💧 From day 2, apply a small amount of brow oil or castor oil at night. This nourishes the brow hairs and helps maintain the softness and shape of the lamination.
☀️ Avoid prolonged sun exposure on the brow area without SPF — UV can fade a tint faster and dry out the brow hairs.
✂️ Only tidy stray hairs with tweezers once you are confident the lamination has fully set (2–3 days). Avoid heavy tweezing — let Stefani shape them at your next visit.
📅 A brow lamination typically lasts 6–8 weeks. Come back no sooner than 6 weeks for your next treatment to keep the hairs healthy.
Brow Shape & Tint / Intro Brows: If you had a tint-only or shape and tint treatment (no lamination), the main rule is the same — keep brows dry for 24 hours and avoid exfoliating acids in the brow area. The tint will fade gradually over 3–5 weeks, more quickly with exfoliation and sun exposure.
