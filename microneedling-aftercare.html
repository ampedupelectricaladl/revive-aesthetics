PDRN Microneedling Aftercare

Post-Treatment Guide

Your skin has just been through something really powerful. A little redness and sensitivity today is completely normal — it means your skin is doing exactly what it should. Give it the right environment to heal and the results will be beautiful.

Stefani x

After your treatment
First 24 hours — most important
  • ✕ No makeup — the micro-channels in your skin are still open and need to stay clean. Let your skin breathe for at least 24 hours
  • ✕ No touching or pressing the treated area with unwashed hands — keep the skin as clean as possible while it heals
  • ✕ No strenuous exercise or anything that causes significant sweating — sweat on freshly needled skin can irritate and introduce bacteria
  • ✕ Avoid heat — saunas, steam rooms, hot showers directed at the face, and hot yoga
  • ✕ No swimming — pool chlorine and water can irritate the skin and increase infection risk in the first 24 hours
  • ✓ If your skin feels tight or warm, a cool (not cold) clean cloth held gently against it can help ease the feeling
  • ✓ Apply any post-treatment products Stefani has recommended — only use what she has given you or approved for this period
Days 2–5
  • ✕ No active skincare — avoid retinol, vitamin C, AHAs, BHAs, and any exfoliating acids for at least 5 days. These are too stimulating for skin that is still healing
  • ✕ No physical exfoliation — no scrubs, cleansing brushes, or facial cloths on the treated area
  • ✕ Don't pick, peel, or scratch any dry or flaking skin — this is a normal part of the healing process and peeling it away early can affect your results
  • ✓ Cleanse gently with a mild, fragrance-free cleanser and lukewarm water — no rubbing, just soft patting motions
  • ✓ Keep skin well hydrated with a gentle, fragrance-free moisturiser — hydration is key for healing and results
  • ✓ Apply SPF 50+ every single morning — your skin is more vulnerable to UV damage during healing, and sun exposure can affect pigmentation and results
Days 5–14
  • ✓ You can gradually reintroduce your regular skincare from around day 5-7, starting with the gentler products first
  • ✓ Keep up the SPF daily — this is non-negotiable for the full two weeks post-treatment
  • ✓ Results continue to develop over 2–4 weeks as the PDRN stimulates collagen production — be patient, the best is still to come

What to expect

🌸 Redness and a warm, sunburnt feeling on the day of treatment is completely normal. Most clients see significant settling within 12–24 hours, though some mild pinkness can continue for 2–3 days.
✨ Some light flaking or dry patches around days 3–5 is part of the healing process. Resist the urge to peel — keep the skin moisturised and let it shed naturally.
🌿 PDRN works by stimulating your skin's own repair process. Visible improvements in texture, tone, and glow typically show at 2–4 weeks, with full collagen remodelling results at 8–12 weeks.
📅 For the best long-term results, a course of treatments is recommended. Stefani will advise on the right spacing for your skin — typically 4–6 weeks between sessions.
☀️ SPF is your best friend after microneedling — not just this week, but always. Protecting your investment in your skin makes every result last longer.
Some bruising, pinpoint bleeding, or swelling immediately after treatment is normal with microneedling and will resolve quickly. If you experience any signs of infection (increasing redness, warmth, pus, or fever), or a reaction that is getting worse rather than better after 48 hours, please contact Stefani or seek medical advice promptly.
