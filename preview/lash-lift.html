What is a Korean Lash Lift?

A premium lash lift using multiple rod sizes to create a natural root lift effect, tighter at the base and looser toward the tips.

Why It's Different
  • Regular lifts curl lashes evenly with one rod size
  • Korean lifts lift from the base, preserving natural length through the tips
  • Eyes look more open and lifted, without the curled-up appearance
  • Especially flattering for straight, downward-growing or hooded lash types
How it Works
  • Smaller rods at the lash base create a tight root curl
  • Larger rods along the length create a gentler curve toward the tips
  • Finished with a keratin boost for shine, strength and longer hold
Why Book It?

Wide-awake eyes without mascara or a curler. Lasts 6-8 weeks with proper aftercare. No extensions, no adhesives, no daily upkeep.
