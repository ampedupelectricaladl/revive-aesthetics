Post - Treatment Aftercare

Korean Lash Lift

Your lashes have just been beautifully lifted and set. The next 24–48 hours
are critical for locking in your results — please follow these guidelines carefully.

Recovery Timeline

0–24 hrs Keep lashes completely dry. No water, steam, sweat or humidity. The lifting solution is still setting — any moisture now can cause lashes to drop or lose their shape.
24–48 hrs Lashes are settling into their new shape. Continue to be gentle — avoid rubbing your eyes and try to sleep on your back where possible.
48–72 hrs Your lift is fully set. You can resume normal cleansing and carefully apply mascara if desired.
Week 2–8 Enjoy your lifted lashes! Nourish them with a lash serum or conditioning oil to keep them healthy. Results last 6–8 weeks.

Do's & Don'ts

✓ Do

  • Keep lashes dry for the first 24 hours
  • Sleep on your back or use a silk pillowcase
  • Gently comb lashes upward daily with a spoolie
  • Apply a nourishing lash serum or castor oil
  • Use an oil-free cleanser gently around the eyes
  • Wear mascara after 48 hours if desired

✕ Don't

  • Get lashes wet for the first 24 hours (no swimming, steam or sweating)
  • Rub or pull at your lashes
  • Use oil-based makeup removers around the eyes
  • Use waterproof mascara — very difficult to remove without tugging
  • Sleep face-down in the first 48 hours
  • Use an eyelash curler — your lashes are already lifted!

When to Contact Us

Some mild redness or sensitivity around the eye area is normal and will settle quickly. If you experience prolonged irritation, unusual swelling, or any unexpected reaction to the treatment, please reach out straight away.
