The treatment

What is a Korean Lash Lift?

A Korean Lash Lift is a semi-permanent treatment that lifts and curls your own natural lashes from the root — not the mid-shaft like a traditional perm. The result is a wide-eyed, fanned effect that makes your lashes look significantly longer and fuller.

The tint is applied immediately after to deepen the colour, making every individual lash visible and defined without any mascara.

It's gentle on natural lashes when done correctly, with no damage and no extensions involved.

Step by step

What happens during your appointment

  1. 1Your lashes are cleansed and a small silicone shield is placed on your eyelid, matched to your lash length and the curl you want.
  2. 2Your lashes are lifted onto the shield, then a gentle lifting solution is applied and left to process.
  3. 3A setting solution locks your lashes in their new lifted shape.
  4. 4A tint is applied to deepen and define the colour of every lash.
  5. 5A nourishing treatment is applied to condition and hydrate your lashes after processing.
The whole appointment takes around 75 minutes. You'll be lying comfortably with your eyes closed for most of it.
Results

What to expect & how long it lasts

Results are immediate — you'll see the difference as soon as you open your eyes. The lift lasts 6–8 weeks, gradually relaxing as your natural lashes grow out.

Duration 6–8 weeks
Appointment 75 min

After 3 weeks, a tint touch-up can refresh the colour as your lashes grow. A full new lift is recommended no sooner than 6 weeks after your previous appointment.
Is it for me?

Who is it suited to

The Korean Lash Lift works beautifully on most natural lash types — short, long, straight, or downward-pointing lashes all respond well. The shield size is chosen specifically for your lash length to give the most flattering result for your eye shape.

It is not suitable if you currently have lash extensions (they need to be removed first) or if you have had a lash lift within the last 6 weeks.

Please let Stefani know if you have any eye conditions, sensitivities, or are pregnant, so your suitability can be confirmed before your booking.

Questions

Frequently asked

Does it damage my natural lashes?
When performed correctly with the right solutions, a lash lift does not damage natural lashes. It's important not to lift more frequently than every 6 weeks to allow the lash cycle to complete.
Can I still wear mascara?
Yes — most clients find they don't need it, but you can wear non-waterproof mascara 48 hours after your appointment. Avoid waterproof formulas as they require an oil-based remover that shortens the lift's longevity.
What do I need to avoid beforehand?
In the 48 hours before your appointment, avoid waterproof mascara, oil-based eye products, eyelash curlers, and heavy gym sessions or saunas. Come to your appointment with clean, dry lashes and no eye makeup.
What about the 48 hours after?
Keep your lashes dry and avoid steam, swimming, heavy sweating, mascara, and oil-based products around the eye area for the first 48 hours. This is what sets the lift for maximum longevity.
What is the Keratin Lash Infusion add-on?
An optional nourishing treatment added at the end of your lift that deeply conditions the lash structure with keratin. It helps maintain strength, shine, and longevity — particularly great if your lashes are fine or have been through extensions before.
